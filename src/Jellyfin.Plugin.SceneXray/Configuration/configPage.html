X-Ray

Shows who is in the current scene and what song is playing. Press X during playback, or use the panel on the left when the controls are visible.

What to analyse

None ticked means every movie and TV library. The Analyse library task (Scheduled Tasks → X-Ray) works through them overnight, newest first.
Titles (or series) in the Animation or Anime genre. Music is still identified.
An episode takes a few minutes, a film around 10–20. X-Ray appears in the player once it is done.
New movies and episodes are analysed once their metadata is in, ahead of the nightly backlog, so they have X-Ray before anyone watches them.

Faces

Downloaded once from the InsightFace project (research licence, personal use).
Many times faster on a supported GPU. Intel GPUs need a one-off 70 MB download (Intel's OpenVINO runtime). Other GPUs (NVIDIA, AMD) fall back to the CPU.
CPU threads for face and voice recognition when they run on the CPU, for each title being analysed. Keep it low to leave room for streaming.
How many titles are analysed at the same time (1 to 4). The models are loaded once and shared; each extra title adds a video decode and its CPU work. 2 suits a GPU with room to spare. Someone pressing play still jumps the queue.
Falls back to the CPU automatically if the GPU cannot decode a file.
Extra actor photos (different ages and looks) make recognition much better, especially for older films. Left empty, X-Ray uses the key Jellyfin's own TMDb provider uses.
A face recognised with high confidence becomes an extra reference for that actor, which helps later episodes and sequels. Faces you name by hand always count.
Advanced tuning
Higher: fewer names, fewer mistakes.
Accepts weaker matches only when they clearly beat every other actor. 0 turns this off.

Voices

Places voice actors (cartoons) and costumed or off-screen characters in their scenes. Voices are named automatically from subtitle speaker tags, from names said in the dialogue, and from voices learned in your other titles. Needs text subtitles.
Same GPUs as faces, set separately. Each subtitle line takes milliseconds instead of a few hundred on a GPU; the first title after a restart spends about 20 seconds preparing the GPU.

Music

Songs in your Jellyfin music libraries are recognised for free once the Index music library task has run. Music is matched by audio fingerprint (Chromaprint) rather than a neural network, so it always runs on the CPU; it takes seconds per title.
Identifies songs you don't own via audd.io (paid per request). Moments the subtitles mark with ♪ are tried first.

Sharing

When this is on, your server sends what it learns about each title to the X-Ray sharing service, and gets other servers' results so it can skip analysing titles they already did.

Sent: which film or episode (its TMDb/IMDb/TVDb ids, edition and runtime), the TMDb ids of the people found and when they appear, scene boundaries, songs found, the X-Ray version, and short audio fingerprints used to line results up with other copies of the same title. For faces X-Ray couldn't name in films and episodes, a face signature (a list of numbers describing the face, not a picture) is sent so other servers' results can name them. Those signatures are never shown to anyone, never sent to other servers, and are deleted once the face is named.

Never sent: pictures of faces, voice data, anything from home videos or other titles without a TMDb or IMDb id, file names or paths, users, or what anyone watched.

The service will know which titles your server has. Sharing works both ways: you can't receive without contributing, or contribute without receiving. Turning this off stops all sending and receiving immediately.

When someone starts watching an extended or theatrical cut nobody shared yet, X-Ray can show the shared data of another cut for the scenes the two have in common. The title is still analysed here later.
Sharing is for invited servers only. Paste the invite you were given (it starts with xray1.), tick the box above and save.

Queues every title that came from the sharing service for analysis on this server, replacing the shared copy.
Leave deletes this server's membership, credit, the list of titles it has, and every face signature it sent; its past results stay as anonymous votes. Leave and withdraw also deletes every result it contributed. Titles already received stay on this server either way.
